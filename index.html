The Philosophy

Culinary composition as pure, unfiltered expression.

Founded in 2018 by Chef Julian Moreau-Vance, AURÉLIA was envisioned not merely as a dining room, but as a culinary studio. Every dish questions the convention between traditional French precision and untamed Nordic foraging traditions.

Our dining sequence respects the diurnal clock. Wild herbs are hand-picked at dawn in the Kentish downs, maritime greens are harvested at low tide, and our sourdough mother culture has matured uninterrupted for thirteen years.
